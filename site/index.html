marimo-flow

Physics-informed neural networks with PINA, a multi-agent team that composes and trains them, and MLflow tracking, in reactive marimo notebooks.

pip install marimo-flow

Examples

  1. PINN solver with MLflow tracking

    Poisson, heat or wave equation (default: Poisson on the unit square) solved with a PINN or SAPINN, runs logged to MLflow, plus an Optuna sweep. Training starts from a button, so this page shows the setup, not trained results.

    Rendered notebookSource

  2. 3D lid-driven cavity

    Incompressible Navier–Stokes in a unit cube, composed from a declarative ProblemSpec: the spatial domain, training metrics and the ux field on the mid-plane.

    Rendered notebookSource

  3. Model-predictive control of a heat rod

    A PINN surrogate of a 1D heat rod drives a rolling-horizon MPC loop on a finite-difference plant; an offset-free observer estimates the surrogate's bias so the plant settles on the setpoint.

    Rendered notebookSource

These are static snapshots rendered in CI with the default parameters, so the controls on each page are inert. To run a notebook interactively, clone the repository and open it with uvx marimo edit --sandbox examples/<name>.py; the notebook declares its own dependencies.
